# PRD: V1.5 & V2 Strategic Roadmaps

_MedRAG Extended Evolution Roadmap_  
_Standardized against OnRent Enterprise Governance_

---

## 1. MedRAG v1.5 Scope (Post-MVP Enhancements)

### 1.1 Multi-Modal Clinical Data Ingestion
- **DICOM Imaging Metadata & Radiologist Report Linking**:
  - Ingestion of chest X-ray and CT scan radiologist impressions.
  - Alignment of imaging findings with longitudinal timeline encounters.
- **Tier 2 PDF Parser (Semi-Structured & Scanned Documents)**:
  - Integration of `docTR` open-source OCR and layout engine for scanned lab sheets.
  - Confidence scoring heuristics fallback between `pdfplumber` and `docTR`.

### 1.2 Automated Clinical Coding & Billing Audit
- **ICD-10-CM & CPT Suggestion Engine**:
  - Mapping of clinical claims and encounter diagnoses to validated billing codes.
  - Identification of documentation gaps where severe conditions lack coding specificity.

### 1.3 Enterprise Data Protection & Persistence
- **WORM Storage Integration**:
  - Continuous streaming of LanceDB audit logs to AWS S3 Object Lock or Azure Immutable Blob storage.
  - Daily cryptographic Merkle root hash anchoring.

---

## 2. MedRAG v2.0 Scope (Enterprise Transformation)

### 2.1 Bi-Directional EHR Integration
- **FHIR R4 Writeback Port**:
  - Authenticated export of verified clinical summaries to Epic Systems (SMART on FHIR) and Oracle Cerner.
  - Clinician approval queue prior to EHR note commit.

### 2.2 Institutional Guideline Ingestion Engine
- **Tenant-Specific Literature Hierarchy**:
  - Ingestion of proprietary hospital clinical pathways and antimicrobial stewardship formularies.
  - Dual-index retrieval querying institutional guidance prior to general textbook corpus.

### 2.3 Real-Time Bedside Assist (Ambient Listening Integration)
- **Clinical Dialogue Transcription & Fact Extraction**:
  - Audio stream transcription ingestion via Whisper / Med-Whisper.
  - Real-time fact-checking during patient rounds with instant literature grounding.
